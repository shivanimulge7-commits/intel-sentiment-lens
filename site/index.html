Data and method

  1. Collect: Amazon Reviews 2023 export (Intel, AMD and NVIDIA files, about 650,000 reviews). Reviews are matched to a product by the Amazon listing title; whole laptops, PCs and bundles are excluded so reviews describe the part itself.
  2. Clean and label: normalise text; 4–5★ positive, 3★ neutral, 1–2★ negative.
  3. Model: lexicon + negation baseline here. Swap in TF-IDF + logistic regression or a fine-tuned transformer and compare in the same metrics table.
  4. Analyse: aspect analysis, quarterly trends, keyword extraction, improvement ranking, repeated on every product category.

Downloads: train.csv · test.csv (80/20 split). Regenerate with python3 pipeline/build_real.py.
